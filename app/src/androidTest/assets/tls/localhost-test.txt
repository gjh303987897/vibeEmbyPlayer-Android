localhost-key.pk8 and localhost-cert.der are a public, synthetic test TLS keypair.
Subject/SAN: localhost. RSA 2048 / SHA256withRSA.
It has no access to any real server or user data and is not included in production APKs.
Exported as PKCS8 DER and X509 DER from src/test/resources/localhost-test.p12.
DER avoids Android PKCS12 provider incompatibility with the JDK-generated MAC.
Used to verify HTTPS backup streaming, Digest retries, downgrade rejection and hostname checks.
